# News of Tuesday 24 November 2026

## US blacklists Megaspeed

The US Commerce Department added Singapore-based Megaspeed and six affiliated entities in Singapore, Malaysia and Thailand to the Entity List, meaning exports of US technology now require a licence with a presumption of denial. The firm is suspected of smuggling Nvidia AI chips to China. It is the first addition to the list in 410 days.

## Zelensky urges four-party talks

Speaking by video to a meeting of NATO parliamentarians in Berlin, President Zelensky called for a four-party meeting of Ukraine, Russia, the United States and Europe as early as possible, before Christmas. Kyiv is ready to meet as early as Tuesday.

## Russian jets enter Korea air zone

On Tuesday at 07:52 Korea time, an evacuation warning was issued for Ulleung Island and lifted three minutes later. Several Russian military aircraft entered the Korea Air Defense Identification Zone over the East Sea without violating South Korean airspace; Seoul scrambled jets and summoned the Russian defence attache to protest the same day.

## China sentences spy official to death

An official surnamed Ma was sentenced to death for leaking 35 state secrets to a foreign spy agency for payments of over $150,000 (over 1 million yuan), announced by Beijing's state secrets watchdog.

## Pentagon rebukes Canada

Washington faulted Ottawa's defence shortfalls in a blunt fact sheet: only 41.9% of Canada's air force fleet is serviceable, and Canada's purchase of 88 F-35 jets is under review.

## FIFA names Women's World Cup hosts

The 2031 Women's World Cup, expanded to 48 teams for the first time, will be hosted by the United States, Mexico, Costa Rica and Jamaica, while the 2035 edition goes to the United Kingdom. Both bids were unopposed.

## Nvidia brings back its CEO

Nvidia's board reinstated Jensen Huang as CEO less than two weeks after his ouster, hours before the Megaspeed blacklist landed.

## Seoul summons Russian attache

South Korea lodged a strong protest with the Russian defence attache over the air-zone entry near Ulleung Island.
