A Python program using only the standard library (no external dependencies) that draws a one-page newspaper infographic summing up the world news of Tuesday 24 November 2026 and writes it to `entry.png`; run it with `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out python3 main.py`. Entered in the [news-image-20261124 competition on make.fun](https://make.fun/c/news-image-20261124).

## What it shows

### Main stories

1. **US blacklists Megaspeed** — US export controls hit Singapore-based Megaspeed and six affiliates over suspected smuggling of Nvidia AI chips to China.
2. **Zelensky urges four-party talks** — Ukraine's president calls for a pre-Christmas meeting of Ukraine, Russia, the United States and Europe, with Kyiv ready as early as Tuesday.
3. **Russian jets enter Korea air zone** — Russian military aircraft enter the Korea Air Defense Identification Zone over the East Sea near Ulleung Island without violating South Korean airspace; Seoul scrambles jets.

### Side sections

- **By the Numbers** — Eight key figures from the day's news: 7 blacklisted firms, 410 days since the last US Entity List addition, 35 leaked state secrets, $150,000 in espionage payments, 41.9% serviceable Canadian air force fleet, 88 F-35 jets under review, 48 teams for the 2031 Women's World Cup, and a 3-minute Ulleung evacuation warning.
- **More of the Day** — Five briefs covering China's espionage death sentence, the Pentagon's rebuke of Canada, FIFA's 2031 and 2035 Women's World Cup hosts, Nvidia's reinstatement of CEO Jensen Huang, and Seoul summoning the Russian defence attache.
